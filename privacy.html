You don't have to give us any personal data to browse Lineupr. An email address (or Google sign-in) and a password are needed only if you want an account. We don't make automated decisions about you, and we don't do profiling.

3. Who processes the data for us

We use these service providers. They process data on our behalf under data processing terms, unless noted.

  • Supabase: account, authentication and database. Data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Cloudflare: screenshot storage (R2) and the Turnstile bot check.
  • Vercel: website hosting, request logs and Vercel Web Analytics.
  • Google: Google Analytics, only if you accept cookies.
  • Google sign-in, only if you use it: Google handles the sign-in as its own controller under Google's privacy policy and shares your basic profile with us.

We may disclose data to authorities when the law requires it.

4. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Vercel, Cloudflare and Google are US companies and may process data in the United States. These transfers are based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, under which the providers are certified, and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses as a fallback.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy of it;
  • have incorrect data corrected (you can change your username and password in your profile);
  • have your data erased (you can delete your account yourself in your profile; this removes your personal lineups, bookmarks and screenshots immediately);
  • restrict processing, and object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • receive data you gave us in a machine-readable format (data portability);
  • withdraw your consent to Google Analytics at any time, without affecting processing before the withdrawal.

Email support@nastipe.dev to use these rights. We reply within one month. In complex cases this can be extended by two more months, and we tell you if so. We may ask you to confirm the request from the email address on your account.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Finland this is the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto), tietosuoja.fi.

6. Security

Passwords are stored only as hashes. Private screenshots are kept in a non-public storage bucket and shown only to their owner (and the administrator for moderation) through short-lived signed links. Screenshots are normally re-encoded in your browser before upload, which removes embedded metadata such as camera or GPS details. Animated GIFs, and images that re-encoding wouldn't make smaller, are uploaded as they are, so avoid uploading files that contain metadata you don't want to share.

7. Children

Lineupr is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly let them create accounts. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We show the date of the latest change at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how we use account holders' data, we tell them by email before it takes effect.
