Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Lineupr ("we", "us") is run by naStipe.dev (Business ID 3447010-7), operating lineupr.org, a personal CS2 grenade lineup database. This page explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it. We do not sell your data, and we do not run ads or marketing trackers.

Data we collect

Account data. If you register, we store the email address, username, and password you provide (passwords are hashed, we never see them in plain text) or, if you sign in with Google, the basic profile info Google shares with us (name, email, avatar). This is handled by our authentication provider, Supabase.

Content you create. Lineups, notes and screenshots you add are stored so you (and, for official lineups, other visitors) can view them. Screenshots are stored with Cloudflare R2.

Usage data. If you accept the cookie banner, Google Analytics collects anonymized usage data (pages viewed, approximate location, device type) to help us understand how the site is used. See our Cookie Policy for details and how to opt out.

Technical data. Our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs basic request data (IP address, timestamps) for security and reliability, as most web hosts do.

Why we collect it

  • To create and secure your account, and let you sign in.
  • To store and display the lineups you create.
  • To understand how the site is used, so we can improve it.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your data. We use a small number of service providers to run the site, each acting as a processor on our behalf:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
  • Cloudflare (R2) — screenshot storage.
  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Google Analytics — anonymized usage analytics (only if you accept cookies).
  • Google Sign-In — optional OAuth login, only if you choose to use it.

We may disclose data if required by law.

Your rights

You can review or edit your profile from the app. To request a copy of your data, or to have your account and associated data deleted, email us at support@nastipe.dev. We'll act on verified requests within a reasonable time.

Data retention

We keep account and lineup data for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal data within a reasonable time, except where we must keep records to comply with the law.

Children

Lineupr is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
