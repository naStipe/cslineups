Lineupr (operated by naStipe.dev, Business ID 3447010-7) stores a few small pieces of data in your browser, as cookies or in your browser's local storage. Under Finnish law (Act on Electronic Communications Services 917/2014, section 205) we may store only what is strictly necessary for the service you use without asking. Everything else needs your consent first.

Strictly necessary

These make the site work as you asked and are always on.

Analytics (optional, only with your consent)

If you press "Accept" on the banner, Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC, USA) sets cookies to measure how the site is used: pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser. Nothing from Google Analytics loads before you accept, and the data isn't used for advertising.

You can change your decision at any time. Withdrawing consent deletes the Google Analytics cookies and shows the banner again.

You can also delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings.
